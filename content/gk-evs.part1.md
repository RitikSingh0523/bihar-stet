# General Knowledge (India & Bihar) & Environmental Science

> **What the exam asks (Part III – General):** 10 questions per paper = about 5 General Knowledge + 5 Environmental Science (EVS). GK mixes Bihar facts, Indian history, geography, polity, economy, science-tech, books/awards/sports/days. 2025 papers asked items like Khudai Khidmatgars (**Red Shirts**), Mongol invasions under the Khaljis/Tughluqs, surface temperature inversion, market surplus, fungicides, and current-affairs items (Chandrayaan-3, Olympics host). EVS items: ecosystem/food chains, pollution, greenhouse gases, ozone, laws, conventions, conservation, disasters. Learn the "one-line facts" below; most MCQs are direct recall.

## 1. Bihar at a Glance (Formation, Geography, Rivers, Parks)

### Formation and basic facts
- **Bihar and Orissa** carved out of the Bengal Presidency: announced at Delhi Durbar Dec 1911, effective **22 March 1912** (**Bihar Diwas**). Orissa separated **1 April 1936**. **Jharkhand** carved out **15 Nov 2000** (Bihar lost most minerals/industry).
- Capital **Patna** (ancient Pataliputra/Kusumapura/Azimabad). **High Court at Patna** (est. 1916). Patna University 1917.
- Area **94,163 km²** (12th largest state); Census 2011 population **10.41 crore** (3rd after UP and Maharashtra); density **1,106/km²** (highest among states); sex ratio **918**; literacy **63.82 %** (lowest among states, Kerala highest at ~94 %).
- **38 districts, 9 divisions** (Patna, Tirhut, Saran, Darbhanga, Kosi, Purnia, Bhagalpur, Munger, Magadh). Assembly **243** seats, Legislative Council **75**, Lok Sabha **40**, Rajya Sabha **16**. Bihar is one of the few states with a Legislative Council (Bihar, UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh).
- Neighbours: **UP (west), Jharkhand (south), West Bengal (east), Nepal (north)**. Landlocked.
- Official language Hindi (Urdu second official). **Maithili** in Eighth Schedule (92nd Amendment, 2003). Other: Bhojpuri, Magahi, Angika, Vajjika.
- First CM **Dr Shri Krishna Sinha** ("Bihar Kesari"); first Deputy CM **Anugrah Narayan Sinha**; first woman CM **Rabri Devi** (1997); **Nitish Kumar** is the longest-serving CM. **Karpoori Thakur** ("Jan Nayak") got Bharat Ratna 2024. First Governor after independence **Jairamdas Daulatram**.
- State symbols: animal **Gaur**; bird **House sparrow** (2013); tree **Peepal**; flower **Marigold (Genda)**.
- Largest district by area **West Champaran**; smallest **Sheohar**; most populous **Patna**; highest literacy **Rohtas**, lowest **Purnia** (2011).
- Bihar is the **first state to abolish the zamindari system** (1950 Act) and the first to give **50 % reservation to women in Panchayats** (2006). Prohibition in force from **April 2016**. Caste-based survey results published **2 Oct 2023**.

### Physical features and rivers
- Three zones: **north Bihar plain** (flood-prone, Terai/Bhabar edge), **Ganga plain**, **south Bihar** (Kaimur plateau, Rajgir-Gaya hills, Tal areas). Highest hills: **Someshwar range** (West Champaran, ~880 m); **Kaimur** and **Rajgir** hills in the south.
- Climate monsoonal sub-tropical; soils mostly alluvial (**Bhangar** old, **Khadar** new, **Diara** river islands, **Tal** depressions in Patna–Munger).
- **Ganga** enters Bihar at **Buxar (Chausa)** and flows west to east (~445 km) leaving near Katihar/Rajmahal side.

| River | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Kosi** | "**Sorrow of Bihar**"; from Nepal (Sapt Kosi: Tamur, Arun, Sun Kosi…); joins Ganga near **Kursela (Katihar)**; shifts course; **Bhimnagar barrage**; 2008 Kusaha breach flood |
| **Gandak** | Called **Narayani / Saligrami** in Nepal; joins Ganga opposite Patna near **Hajipur–Sonepur**; **Valmikinagar barrage** |
| **Son** | Rises at **Amarkantak**; right-bank tributary; joins Ganga near **Maner**; **Indrapuri barrage** (Rohtas) |
| **Bagmati, Kamla, Burhi Gandak** | Rise in Nepal/Himalayan foothills; flood north Bihar |
| **Mahananda** | From Darjeeling; Kishanganj/Katihar |
| **Falgu (Phalgu)** | Formed by Lilajan + Mohana at **Gaya**; sacred for **pind-daan** |
| **Karmanasa** | Kaimur hills; boundary of Bihar and UP ("cursed river") |
| **Punpun, Kiul, Badua, Chandan** | South-bank tributaries of Ganga |
| **Ghaghra (Saryu)** | Left-bank; meets Ganga near Chhapra (Saran) |

- **Sonepur Mela** (Harihar Kshetra, Kartik Purnima) at Ganga–Gandak confluence – among Asia's biggest cattle fairs.

### Bridges and institutions
- **Gandhi Setu** (Patna–Hajipur, 1982); **Rajendra Setu** (Mokama, 1959); **Vikramshila Setu** (Bhagalpur); **JP Setu** (Digha–Sonepur rail-cum-road). **Golghar** Patna (1786, John Garstin, granary). Khuda Bakhsh Oriental Library (1891). **Barauni** refinery (IOC, 1964). **Kahalgaon** NTPC (Bhagalpur). East Central Railway HQ **Hajipur**.
- Institutions: IIT Patna, AIIMS Patna, NIT Patna, **Nalanda University** (revived by 2010 Act; new Rajgir campus inaugurated 2024).

### Wildlife of Bihar

| Area | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Valmiki National Park / Tiger Reserve** | West Champaran; **only NP and only tiger reserve of Bihar**; NP 1989, TR 1990 |
| **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary** | Bhagalpur (Sultanganj–Kahalgaon stretch); only dolphin sanctuary in Asia (1991) |
| **Kaimur WLS** | Largest WLS of Bihar |
| **Gautam Buddha WLS** (Gaya), **Rajgir (Pant) WLS**, **Bhimbandh WLS** (Munger), **Udaipur WLS** (West Champaran) | Other sanctuaries |
| **Kanwar (Kabartal) Lake** | Begusarai; Asia's largest freshwater oxbow lake; Bihar's first Ramsar wetland |
| **Kusheshwar Asthan** | Bird sanctuary, Darbhanga |

- **Gangetic dolphin (Susu)** is the National Aquatic Animal (2009); Project Dolphin launched 2020.
- Bihar produces most of India's **litchi** (Shahi litchi, Muzaffarpur), **makhana (Mithila Makhana, GI)**; major maize, paddy, wheat; Bhagalpur **Zardalu mango, Katarni rice (GI)**, Maghi paan.

**⚡ Exam tip:** Remember pairs: Kosi = Sorrow of Bihar; Gandak = Narayani; Valmiki = only tiger reserve; Vikramshila = dolphin; Bihar Diwas = 22 March; Jharkhand split = 15 Nov 2000.

## 2. Bihar – History and Freedom Movement

### Ancient Bihar (Magadha)
- **Mahajanapadas**: Magadha, Anga (Champa/Bhagalpur), Vajji/Vaishali (Lichchhavi confederacy = first republic). Magadha capitals: **Rajagriha/Girivraja → Pataliputra** (moved by Udayin; Patna).
- **Haryanka**: Bimbisara (contemporary of Buddha; annexed Anga), Ajatashatru (used catapult/rathamusala; 1st Buddhist Council), Udayin. **Shishunaga**, then **Nanda** (Mahapadma Nanda "sarvakshatrantaka"; last Dhanananda).
- **Mauryas**: **Chandragupta Maurya 322 BCE** (with **Chanakya/Kautilya**; *Arthashastra*); **Bindusara**; **Ashoka** (Kalinga war 261 BCE; Dhamma; 3rd Buddhist Council at Pataliputra; Ashokan pillars at **Lauriya Nandangarh, Lauriya Araraj, Vaishali** with lion capital). **Megasthenes** (*Indica*) lived at Pataliputra. **Kumhrar** (Patna) has Mauryan pillared-hall remains.
- **Buddhism/Jainism**: Buddha attained enlightenment at **Bodh Gaya** (Mahabodhi temple, UNESCO 2002); last sermon at Vaishali; 1st Council at **Rajgir** (Sattapanni cave); 2nd at **Vaishali**; 3rd at **Pataliputra**. **Mahavira** born at Kundagrama (Vaishali), died at **Pawapuri** (Nalanda dist.). **Kesaria stupa** (East Champaran) – among the largest Buddhist stupas.
- **Guptas** (Pataliputra): Chandragupta I (Gupta era 319–20 CE), **Samudragupta** ("Napoleon of India", V.A. Smith), **Chandragupta II Vikramaditya** (Fa-Hien visited), **Kumaragupta I** founded **Nalanda Mahavihara** (UNESCO 2016). **Aryabhata** worked at Kusumapura (Patna); *Aryabhatiya* 499 CE.
- **Palas** (Bengal–Bihar, founder **Gopala**): **Dharmapala** founded **Vikramshila Mahavihara** (Antichak, Bhagalpur); Odantapuri (Bihar Sharif). **Bakhtiyar Khalji** destroyed Nalanda, Vikramshila and Odantapuri (~1193–1203). Xuanzang studied at Nalanda; Itsing also visited.
- **Sita** born at Sitamarhi; **Janaka**'s Mithila (Videha); **Vidyapati** (Maithili poet, "Maithil Kokil") from Bisfi (Madhubani).
- **Guru Gobind Singh** born at Patna (1666): **Takht Sri Harmandir Sahib (Patna Sahib)**.

### Medieval and colonial Bihar
- **Sher Shah Suri** (born Sasaram; defeated Humayun at **Chausa 1539**, Kannauj 1540); built Grand Trunk Road, introduced rupee/dam; **tomb at Sasaram** (lake). **Babur** fought Afghans of Bihar at **Ghaghra (1529)**.
- **Battle of Buxar, 22 Oct 1764** (Hector Munro beat Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-daula, Shah Alam II) → **Treaty of Allahabad 1765**: Diwani of Bengal-Bihar-Orissa to the Company.
- **Wahabi** movement centre at **Patna (Sadiqpur)**.

### Freedom struggle in Bihar

| Event/Person | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Tilka Manjhi** | Led Paharia/Santhal rebellion near Bhagalpur 1780s; hanged 1785 |
| **Revolt of 1857** | **Danapur** troops mutinied 25 July 1857; **Pir Ali Khan** (Patna bookseller) hanged; **Babu Kunwar Singh** (Jagdishpur, Arrah; ~80 years old) led, defeated British at Arrah/Azamgarh, died **26 April 1858** (Vijayotsav 23 April) |
| **Champaran Satyagraha 1917** | **Gandhi's first satyagraha in India**, against **tinkathia** (indigo) system; invited by **Raj Kumar Shukla**; helpers **Rajendra Prasad, Brajkishore Prasad, Mazharul Haque, Anugrah Narayan Sinha, J.B. Kripalani**; **Champaran Agrarian Act 1918** |
| **Non-Cooperation** | Sadaqat Ashram (Mazharul Haque), Bihar Vidyapith (Patna) |
| **Kisan Sabha** | **Swami Sahajanand Saraswati**, Bihta, 1929 (Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha) |
| **1934 earthquake** | 15 Jan 1934 (8.0) devastated north Bihar |
| **1937** | Congress ministry; **Shri Krishna Sinha** premier |
| **Quit India 1942** | **11 Aug 1942**: 7 students shot while hoisting flag at the Secretariat (**Shaheed Smarak**, Patna); **Jayaprakash Narayan** escaped Hazaribagh jail |

- **Dr Rajendra Prasad** (born Ziradei, Siwan): chairman of the Constituent Assembly, **first President of India** (1950–62), Bharat Ratna 1962. **Dr Sachchidananda Sinha** was the **interim president** of the Constituent Assembly (Dec 1946). **Babu Jagjivan Ram** (Arrah), Deputy PM 1977. **Lok Nayak JP** (Bharat Ratna 1998): **Sampoorna Kranti (Total Revolution), 1974** launched from Patna's Gandhi Maidan. **Birsa Munda** (Ulgulan 1899–1900) tribal leader of Chhotanagpur (then Bihar).
- INC 1912 session held at **Bankipore (Patna)**. **Satyendra Prasanno Sinha** first Indian Governor (Bihar & Orissa, 1920).

**⚡ Exam tip:** Champaran 1917 = first Gandhian satyagraha in India (Kheda 1918, Ahmedabad 1918 followed). Kunwar Singh = Jagdishpur/Arrah, 1857. Nalanda = Kumaragupta; Vikramshila = Dharmapala.

## 3. Bihar – Culture, Festivals and Government Schemes

### Art and craft

| Art | Place/Fact |
|---|---|
| **Madhubani (Mithila) painting** | Mithila region; GI tag; natural colours, double line border; styles Bharni, Katchni, Tantrik, Godna, Kohbar. Padma awardees: **Sita Devi, Ganga Devi, Mahasundari Devi, Baua Devi, Dulari Devi** |
| **Manjusha art** | **Bhagalpur** (Anga); "snake painting", Bihula–Vishahari legend |
| **Sujini embroidery** | **Bhusura, Muzaffarpur** |
| **Tikuli art** | Patna (painted glass/hardboard) |
| **Applique (Khatwa)** | Bhagalpur/Patna |
| **Sikki grass** craft | Mithila |
| **Patna Kalam** | Company school of painting, 18th–19th century |
| **Bhagalpuri silk** | Bhagalpur = "**Silk City**" (Tussar) |

### Folk music, dance, literature
- Folk theatre **Bidesiya** by **Bhikhari Thakur** ("**Shakespeare of Bhojpuri**", Kutubpur, Saran); plays *Bidesiya, Beti Bechwa, Gabarghichor*. Other: **Jat-Jatin, Jhijhiya, Sama-Chakeva, Kajri, Sohar, Chaita, Alha-Udal (Bhojpuri ballad), Domkach, Launda naach, Karma, Jhumar**.
- **Ustad Bismillah Khan** (Dumraon, Buxar; shehnai; Bharat Ratna 2001).
- Authors: **Ramdhari Singh Dinkar** (Simaria, Begusarai; *Urvashi* – Jnanpith 1972; *Kurukshetra, Rashmirathi, Parashuram ki Pratiksha*); **Phanishwar Nath Renu** (*Maila Anchal*, *Parti Parikatha*; Araria; "Teesri Kasam" film); **Nagarjuna** (Baba Nagarjuna; Maithili name Yatri; Sahitya Akademi 1969); **Gopal Singh Nepali**; **Shivpujan Sahay**; **Vidyapati** (*Padavali, Kirtilata*). **Rahul Sankrityayan** (*Volga se Ganga*) worked in Bihar.

### Festivals and fairs
- **Chhath** (Kartik Shukla Shashthi; 4 days: Nahay-khay, Kharna, Sandhya Arghya, Usha Arghya): worship of **Surya and Chhathi Maiya**; offering **Thekua**.
- **Sama-Chakeva** (brother–sister, Mithila), **Madhushravani** (newly married brides, Shravan), **Jur Sital** (Maithili new year, 14–15 April), **Jitiya** (mothers for children), **Teej**, **Phagua** (Holi), **Pitripaksha Mela** (Gaya), **Sonepur Mela**, **Rajgir Mahotsav**, **Vaishali Mahotsav** (Mahavir Jayanti).
- Food: **Litti-Chokha, Sattu, Khaja (Silao), Tilkut (Gaya), Anarsa, Thekua, Makhana kheer, Lal Peda (Barh?), Malpua**. Famous: **Maner ke Ladoo** (Maner Sharif, Patna).

### Heritage sites
**Mahabodhi (Bodh Gaya), Nalanda ruins (both UNESCO)**; Vishnupad temple (Gaya); Vishwa Shanti Stupa and hot springs (Rajgir); Jal Mandir (Pawapuri); **Mundeshwari temple (Kaimur, ancient octagonal)**; Janaki temple (Sitamarhi); Rohtasgarh fort (Rohtas); **Patna Sahib**; Bihar Museum, Patna Museum (1917).

### Bihar government schemes (commonly asked)
- **Saat Nischay** (Seven Resolves, 2015; part 2 in 2020): youth skilling and **Student Credit Card**, women's reservation in jobs, **Har Ghar Bijli, Har Ghar Nal ka Jal**, pucca lanes and drains, toilet at every home, further education.
- **Mukhyamantri Balika Cycle & Poshak Yojana** (2006–07) for girls; **Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana** (2018); **JEEViKA** (Bihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society, 2006, World Bank-supported; women SHGs "Didi"); **Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyan** (2019); **Lohia Swachh Bihar Abhiyan**; **Bihar Student Credit Card** (2016).
- National schemes: **PM-KISAN**, **Jan Dhan**, **Ayushman Bharat**, **Swachh Bharat** (2 Oct 2014), **Ujjwala**, **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao**.

**⚡ Exam tip:** Map artform to place: Madhubani = Mithila; Manjusha = Bhagalpur; Sujini = Bhusura; Tikuli = Patna; Bidesiya = Bhikhari Thakur; Chhath = Surya.

## 4. Indian History – Timeline and High-yield Facts

### Ancient India
- **Indus Valley**: Harappa (Daya Ram Sahni, 1921), Mohenjodaro (R.D. Banerji, 1922); **Lothal** dockyard; **Dholavira** (Gujarat; UNESCO 2021); Kalibangan (Rajasthan). Bronze age, town planning, no iron.
- **Vedic**: Rigveda earliest (1028 hymns, Sapta-Sindhu); later Vedic – Sabha, Samiti; Varna system.
- **Jainism**: 24 Tirthankaras – Rishabhanatha first, **Parshvanatha** 23rd, **Mahavira** 24th; Triratna. **Buddhism**: Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path; born Lumbini; first sermon Sarnath; died Kushinagar.
- **Alexander** invaded 326 BCE (Porus, Jhelum). **Mauryas** 322–185 BCE (see Bihar). **Prinsep** deciphered Brahmi (1837).
- **Kushanas**: Kanishka (Saka era 78 CE; Gandhara art). **Satavahanas**: Gautamiputra Satakarni. **Sangam age**: Cholas, Cheras, Pandyas.
- **Guptas**: Allahabad Prashasti by **Harishena**; Kalidasa; Iron pillar Mehrauli; decimal/zero.
- **Harsha** (606–647, Kannauj; *Harshacharita* by Banabhatta; defeated by **Pulakeshin II** on Narmada). **Cholas**: Rajaraja I (Brihadeeswara, Thanjavur), Rajendra I (Gangaikonda Cholapuram); Uttaramerur inscriptions. **Rashtrakutas**: Kailasa temple at Ellora.

### Modern India
- **1498** Vasco da Gama (Calicut); **1600** East India Company; **Plassey 1757** (Clive vs Siraj-ud-daula; betrayal by Mir Jafar); **Buxar 1764**. **Wellesley**: Subsidiary Alliance; **Dalhousie**: **Doctrine of Lapse** (Satara 1848, Jhansi 1853, Nagpur 1854), first railway (Bombay–Thane 1853), telegraph, Wood's Despatch 1854.
- **1857 Revolt**: Mangal Pandey (Barrackpore, 29 Mar); Meerut 10 May; Delhi (Bahadur Shah Zafar); Kanpur (Nana Sahib, Tatya Tope); Lucknow (Begum Hazrat Mahal); Jhansi (Rani Lakshmibai); Bihar (Kunwar Singh). Governor-General **Canning**. **Government of India Act 1858**: Crown rule; Queen's Proclamation 1 Nov 1858.
- Social reform: **Brahmo Samaj 1828 (Raja Ram Mohan Roy)**, Prarthana Samaj 1867, **Arya Samaj 1875 (Dayanand; *Satyarth Prakash*)**, **Ramakrishna Mission 1897 (Vivekananda)**, Theosophical Society (Annie Besant), Satyashodhak Samaj 1873 (Phule), Aligarh (Sir Syed).
- Viceroys: Ripon (local self-government, Ilbert Bill), Lytton (Vernacular Press Act 1878), **Curzon (Partition of Bengal 1905)**, Mountbatten last Viceroy; **C. Rajagopalachari** last Governor-General.
