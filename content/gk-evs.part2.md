## 5. Geography of India

### Location and extent
- Area **3.287 million km²** (7th largest); latitudes **8°4′N–37°6′N**, longitudes **68°7′E–97°25′E**. **Standard Meridian 82°30′E** (near Mirzapur, UP) → IST = GMT + 5:30.
- **Tropic of Cancer (23½°N)** crosses **8 states**: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram. (Bihar is not on it.)
- Coastline **7,516 km** (incl. islands); **Gujarat** longest coastline among states. **28 states, 8 UTs**. Neighbours: Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar (+ Sri Lanka, Maldives by sea).
- Extremes: **Indira Point** (Great Nicobar, southernmost point of India); Kanyakumari = southern tip of the mainland; Kibithu (east), Guhar Moti (west), Indira Col (north).
- Largest state by area **Rajasthan**; smallest **Goa**; most populous **UP**; **Sikkim** least populous. India 2011: population 121 crore, literacy 74.04 %, sex ratio 943.

### Physiography
- **Himalayas**: Greater (Himadri; Everest, **Kanchenjunga 8,586 m – highest in India**), Lesser (Himachal), Shiwaliks; **Nanda Devi (7,816 m) highest peak lying wholly in India**; K2/Godwin Austen highest in the Indian-claimed territory (PoK).
- **Northern plains**: Bhabar (pebble belt) → Terai (marshy) → **Bhangar** (old alluvium) → **Khadar** (new alluvium).
- **Peninsular plateau**: Malwa, **Chotanagpur**, Deccan (lava; black soil). **Aravallis** – oldest fold mountains; highest peak **Guru Shikhar** (Mt Abu).
- **Western Ghats (Sahyadri)**: continuous, Tapti to Kanyakumari, ~1,600 km, UNESCO site 2012, **Anamudi (2,695 m)** is highest in peninsular India; **Doddabetta (Nilgiris, 2,637 m)**; passes **Thal, Bhor, Pal Ghat (Palakkad Gap)**.
- **Eastern Ghats**: discontinuous, broken by Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri; highest peak **Jindhagada / Arma Konda (~1,690 m, Andhra Pradesh)**; **Mahendragiri (~1,501 m, Odisha–Andhra)** is another prominent peak (do not confuse with ISRO's Propulsion Complex of the same name in Tamil Nadu). Eastern Ghats = Nallamala, Javadi, Shevaroy, Palkonda hills. Western and Eastern Ghats meet at the **Nilgiris**.
- **Passes**: Banihal (Jammu–Srinagar), **Zoji La** (Srinagar–Leh), **Shipki La** (HP–Tibet), **Nathu La** (Sikkim–Tibet), Lipulekh (Uttarakhand–Tibet; Kailash Mansarovar route), **Khardung La**, Bara-Lacha La, Rohtang.
- **Islands**: **Andaman** (Barren Island – only active volcano; Saddle Peak) and **Nicobar** separated by the **Ten Degree Channel**; **Lakshadweep** (coral atolls; capital Kavaratti; Eight Degree Channel separates Minicoy from Maldives).
- **Thar desert** (Rajasthan; Luni river). **Chilika** – largest brackish lagoon (Odisha). **Sambhar** – largest inland saline lake. **Wular** – largest freshwater lake. **Loktak** (Manipur, floating phumdis). **Vembanad** – longest lake in India (Kerala).

- Dams: **Bhakra-Nangal (Sutlej; Gobind Sagar)**, **Hirakud** (longest earthen), **Tehri** (tallest, Bhagirathi), Nagarjuna Sagar; **Farakka barrage** (1975) on Ganga.

### Climate, soils, crops, minerals
- Four IMD seasons: winter (Jan–Feb), pre-monsoon/summer (Mar–May), **SW monsoon (Jun–Sep)**, retreating monsoon (Oct–Dec). Monsoon onset over Kerala ~1 June. **Western disturbances** → winter rain in NW India (good for rabi wheat). **Loo** (hot winds, plains), **Kalbaisakhi/Nor'wester** (Bengal, Bihar, Assam), **Mango showers** (Kerala/Karnataka).
- **Mawsynram** (Meghalaya) highest rainfall; **Jaisalmer** among the driest. Surface temperature inversion: cold air near ground, warm above → **stable** air (not unstable); usually short-lived, ends after sunrise – ground-level fog/pollution trapping.
- Soils: **Alluvial** (most widespread, ~40 %; rice, wheat; Bihar), **Black/Regur** (Deccan, cotton, high water retention), **Red** (iron oxide), **Laterite** (Kerala, Karnataka; cashew/tea), Desert, Mountain, Saline (reh/kallar).
- Crops: Kharif (rice, maize, cotton, jowar, bajra, sugarcane); Rabi (wheat, gram, mustard, barley, peas); Zaid (watermelon, cucumber). Producers: tea – Assam; coffee – Karnataka; rubber – Kerala; jute – West Bengal; cotton – Gujarat/Maharashtra; sugarcane – UP; wheat – UP; Bihar – litchi, makhana, maize. **Fungicides** (e.g., Bordeaux mixture, sulphur) kill fungus; insecticides kill insects; herbicides weeds.
- Minerals: coal – Jharkhand/Odisha/Chhattisgarh; iron ore – Odisha/Jharkhand/Chhattisgarh/Karnataka; mica – Jharkhand/Andhra; bauxite – Odisha; oil – Mumbai High, Assam (**Digboi** oldest field).
- Forest cover about **21–22 %** of area (target 33 % per National Forest Policy 1988); largest forest area MP; highest percentage Mizoram.

**⚡ Exam tip:** Anamudi > Doddabetta; Kanchenjunga = highest in India, Nanda Devi = highest *entirely* in India; Western Ghats continuous vs Eastern Ghats discontinuous; Chilika brackish, Wular freshwater, Sambhar saline.

## 6. Indian Polity and Economy

### Constitution – making and structure
- Constituent Assembly first met **9 Dec 1946** (interim president **Sachchidananda Sinha**; permanent president **Rajendra Prasad**; Drafting Committee chairman **B.R. Ambedkar**; B.N. Rau constitutional adviser). Adopted **26 Nov 1949** (Constitution Day), in force **26 Jan 1950**; took 2 years 11 months 18 days.
- Originally **395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules**; now about **448 Articles, 25 Parts, 12 Schedules**. Preamble: Sovereign, **Socialist, Secular**, Democratic, Republic (words added by **42nd Amendment 1976**). **Kesavananda Bharati (1973)** – basic structure doctrine.
- Borrowed features: UK – parliamentary system, single citizenship, rule of law; USA – Fundamental Rights, judicial review, Vice-President; **Ireland – DPSP**, Rajya Sabha nominations; **Canada – federation with strong centre, residuary powers**; Australia – Concurrent List, joint sitting; **Germany (Weimar) – emergency**; USSR – Fundamental Duties; France – Republic, liberty-equality-fraternity; South Africa – amendment procedure.

### Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Duties

| Topic | Facts |
|---|---|
| **FR – Part III (Art 12–35)** | Equality 14–18 (Art 17 abolishes untouchability); Freedom 19–22 (six freedoms in 19; Art 21 life & liberty; **21A** education 6–14, 86th Amendment 2002); Exploitation 23–24; Religion 25–28; Cultural/Educational 29–30; **Constitutional Remedies 32** ("heart and soul" – Ambedkar) |
| Right to Property | Removed as FR by **44th Amendment 1978**; now legal right **Art 300A** |
| **Writs** | Habeas corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo warranto (SC Art 32; HC Art 226) |
| **DPSP – Part IV (Art 36–51)** | From Ireland; non-justiciable; Art 40 village panchayats; 44 Uniform Civil Code; 48A environment (42nd); 50 separation of judiciary; 51 peace |
| **Fundamental Duties – Part IVA (Art 51A)** | Added by **42nd Amendment 1976** (Swaran Singh Committee); 10 originally, **11th** (education, 6–14) by 86th Amendment 2002; **51A(g)** protect natural environment |

### Union government
- **President** (Art 52): indirectly elected (elected MPs + MLAs; proportional representation by single transferable vote); min age **35**; term 5 years; impeachment Art 61; ordinance Art 123; pardon Art 72. **Vice-President** – ex-officio Chairman of **Rajya Sabha**; elected by MPs of both houses. **PM** Art 74/75; collective responsibility to Lok Sabha (Art 75(3)). **Attorney General** Art 76. **CAG** Art 148.
- **Parliament**: Lok Sabha (max 552; 543 elected; term 5 yrs; min age 25); **Rajya Sabha** (max 250; 12 nominated; permanent; **6-year term**, 1/3 retire every 2 years; min age 30). **Money Bill** (Art 110) only in Lok Sabha; RS can only recommend within 14 days. Joint sitting Art 108 (not for money bills or constitutional amendments). Budget Art 112. Presiding: Speaker (LS). Quorum 1/10.
- **Emergency**: Art 352 (national), **356 (President's rule)**, 360 (financial). **Art 368** amendment. **Art 370** abrogated 5 Aug 2019. Art 324 Election Commission (25 Jan 1950 → National Voters' Day). **Art 280** Finance Commission (every 5 years). NITI Aayog (1 Jan 2015) replaced Planning Commission (1950).
- **Governor** Art 153 (min age 35, appointed by President, holds office at pleasure); CM Art 164. **Judiciary**: SC Art 124 (retirement 65), HC (62); **Art 141** SC law binding; PIL.
- Presidents: Rajendra Prasad (1st), Radhakrishnan, **Zakir Husain** (first to die in office), **Pratibha Patil** (first woman), Kalam, **Droupadi Murmu** (15th, 2022, first tribal woman). PMs: Nehru, Shastri, **Indira Gandhi** (first woman), **Morarji Desai** (first non-Congress), Vajpayee, Manmohan Singh, Narendra Modi.

- **Panchayati Raj**: **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee 1957** (3-tier); first launched at **Nagaur, Rajasthan, 2 Oct 1959**; Ashok Mehta Committee 1977; 73rd Amendment gave constitutional status (in force 24 Apr 1993 → National Panchayati Raj Day). Three tiers: Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zila Parishad; **1/3 seats for women** (Bihar, since 2006, 50 %); Gram Sabha; State Election Commission and State Finance Commission every 5 years.
- **Eighth Schedule: 22 languages**. **RTI Act 2005**; NHRC 1993; Lokpal Act 2013; **NOTA** from 2013.

### Economy basics
- **RBI**: est. **1 April 1935** (nationalised 1949; HQ Mumbai); controls repo rate, CRR, SLR. **SEBI** (1988; statutory 1992); **NABARD** 1982; SBI 1955 (from Imperial Bank). Bank nationalisation 1969 (14 banks), 1980 (6). One-rupee note is issued by the Finance Ministry (Government of India); other notes by RBI.
- Planning: First Five-Year Plan 1951 (agriculture); 2nd (1956, Mahalanobis, heavy industry). Reforms **1991 (LPG)** – PM Narasimha Rao, FM Manmohan Singh. **GST** from 1 July 2017.
- Sectors: **Primary** (agriculture, mining, fishing, forestry), Secondary (manufacturing, construction), **Tertiary** (services; largest share of GDP). **Direct taxes**: income tax, corporate tax; indirect: GST, customs, excise.
- **MSP** recommended by CACP; **FCI** (Food Corporation of India) procures wheat and rice at MSP for PDS/buffer stock. **Green Revolution** – M.S. Swaminathan (Indian father), Norman Borlaug; **White Revolution** (Operation Flood) – Verghese Kurien (Amul).
- Economic terms: **Surplus** = supply > demand (price falls); **Shortage** = demand > supply; **Equilibrium** where they are equal. Financial year **1 April–31 March**; Budget presented on **1 Feb**.

**⚡ Exam tip:** DPSP = Ireland; FD = USSR; Art 32 = remedies; 42nd = Socialist/Secular/Duties; 73rd = Panchayat; 101st = GST; Rajya Sabha chair = Vice-President.

## 7. Science & Tech, Books, Awards, Sports, Days, Symbols, Organisations

### Science and technology
- **ISRO**: INCOSPAR 1962 → ISRO **1969** (Vikram Sarabhai); HQ Bengaluru. **Aryabhata** 1975 (first satellite); **Rohini** (SLV-3, 1980). **Chandrayaan-1** (22 Oct 2008, PSLV-C11; found water molecules); **Mangalyaan (MOM)** launched 5 Nov 2013, Mars orbit **24 Sept 2014** (first attempt success); **Chandrayaan-3** (14 July 2023) landed near the **lunar south pole on 23 Aug 2023** – India first to do so, 4th country to soft-land; **Aditya-L1** (2 Sept 2023) – first Indian solar observatory, at Sun–Earth **L1**; Gaganyaan – human spaceflight programme. **Rakesh Sharma** first Indian in space (1984).
- **DRDO** 1958; **APJ Abdul Kalam** "Missile Man" (Agni, Prithvi, IGMDP). Pokhran-I 1974, Pokhran-II 1998. Tarapur – first nuclear power plant; **BARC** (Homi Bhabha).
- Nobel (India): Tagore 1913 (Lit.), **C.V. Raman 1930** (Physics; Raman effect 28 Feb 1928 → **National Science Day**), Khorana 1968, Mother Teresa 1979 (Peace), Chandrasekhar 1983, Amartya Sen 1998, Venkatraman Ramakrishnan 2009, Kailash Satyarthi 2014 (Peace), Abhijit Banerjee 2019.
- Basics: Vitamins – A (night blindness), B1 (beri-beri), C (scurvy), **D (rickets; synthesised in skin by sunlight)**, K (clotting). Human body: 206 bones; largest organ skin; largest gland liver. Nitrogen ~78 % of air, oxygen ~21 %. Penicillin – Alexander Fleming (1928). Iris controls the amount of light entering the eye. SI units: coulomb (charge), ampere (current), pascal (pressure), hertz. Sea water boils slightly above 100 °C (dissolved salts).
- Disease vectors: malaria – female *Anopheles*; dengue – *Aedes*; TB – bacterium; Green Revolution crops – HYV wheat.

### National symbols
- Flag: tricolour (saffron, white, green), adopted **22 July 1947**, based on a design by Pingali Venkayya; **Ashoka Chakra (24 spokes)**; ratio 3:2. **Emblem**: Lion Capital of Ashoka (Sarnath); motto *Satyameva Jayate* (Mundaka Upanishad). **Anthem** Jana Gana Mana (Tagore; adopted 24 Jan 1950; ~52 s); **Song** Vande Mataram (Bankim).
- National animal **tiger**; bird **peacock**; flower **lotus**; tree **banyan**; fruit **mango**; river **Ganga**; aquatic animal **Gangetic dolphin**; heritage animal elephant; reptile king cobra. Currency symbol ₹ (2010, D. Udaya Kumar). National calendar **Saka** (1957).
